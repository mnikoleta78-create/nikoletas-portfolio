Selected project / 2024

LiveWall

Mach8 is a company that focuses on the application of Gen-AI to create scalable, personalised, and simplified digital contents. This company is part of Livewall, a digital agency that specialises in the development of apps, games, and digital contents. In Livewall, Mach8 mainly specialises in product development that requires Gen-AI. The given assignment revolves around Gen-AI, specifically the application of Gen-AI in online storytelling. The task was to make a digital product that generates stories with AI based on user input, with a theme that revolves around the Dutch theme park: Efteling.

My contrbutions: Target group literature research, AI generated animation research, Target audience research, User Stories, Created a Customer Journey & Empathy Map; Contributed to creating a high-fidelity prototype, Worked on a part of the front end development - specifically the part where the story generation is happening.

  • Role UX/UI · Interaction design
  • Tools Figma · HTML/CSS/JS
  • Focus Research · Visual design · Prototyping
